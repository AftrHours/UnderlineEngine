down go the casks to their final rest in the sea || where once again leviathan returns to his native profundities
you would all but swear you trod some silent merchant vessel || the entire ship seems great leviathan himself
every cask is out of sight || great rusty casks lie about, as in a brewery yard
The soot is brushed from the lower rigging. || the smoke from the try-works has besooted all the bulwarks
To hint to such musked mariners of oil, and bone, and blubber || the mariners go about suffused with unctuousness
make a spotless dairy room of it || issue to the immaculate deck, fresh and all aglow
