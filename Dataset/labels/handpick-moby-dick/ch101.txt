All honor to the Enderbies || the original of the famous whaling house of Enderby & Sons
the original Samuel must long ago have slipped his cable || named after the late Samuel Enderby, merchant of that city
The ship named after him was worthy of the honor || Ere the English ship fades from sight
the Samuel Enderby was a jolly ship; of good fare and plenty; fine flip and strong || drank good flip down in the forecastle
passed round the beef, and the bread, and the can || The beef was fine—tough, but with body in it.
their fat old fashions, touching plenty to eat and drink || The abounding good cheer of these English whalers is matter for historical research.
